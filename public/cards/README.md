# Card illustrations

Drop commissioned artwork here as `<card-id>.webp` (e.g. `zorp.webp`, `ultra-void.webp`),
portrait 3:4, at least 900×1200, subject centred with transparent or full-bleed background.
List the ids that have art in `src/lib/cardArt.ts` and the card frame switches from the
vector placeholder to the illustration automatically.
